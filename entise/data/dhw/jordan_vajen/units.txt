dhw_activity.csv
day_of_week	Weekday number from 0 (Monday) to 6 (Sunday).
time	Local time of day in HH:MM format.
event	Draw-off category (Bath, Medium, Shower or Small).
probability	Dimensionless weight for a draw-off in this time slot.
duration[s]	Mean draw-off duration in seconds.
flow_rate[l s-1]	Mean draw-off flow rate in litres per second.
duration_sigma[s]	Standard deviation of duration in seconds.
flow_rate_sigma[l s-1]	Standard deviation of flow rate in litres per second.
probability_day	Dimensionless weight for the event category on this weekday.

dhw_demand_by_dwelling.csv
dwelling_size[m2]	Reference floor area in square metres.
yearly_dhw_demand[m3 m-2 a-1]	Mean annual hot-water demand in cubic metres per square metre.
sigma	Standard deviation of daily demand as a fraction of its mean.
